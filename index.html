PAPER PLUGIN

Know who you are dealing with.

Firmament is staff tooling for reviewing player accounts. It keeps a quiet record of who has been on your server, surfaces accounts that appear to be connected, and gives your team a way to look at that before making a call.

LOOKUP/alts <player>

OVERVIEW

Built for the people running the server.

Everything it does is for staff. Players never see it, are never gated by it, and are never interrupted by it — it adds nothing to the experience of joining and playing.

review

A view worth reading

Linked accounts are laid out visually rather than as a wall of text, so what connects to what is legible at a glance. Click through to explore related accounts without retyping a command.

notify

Told, not buried

Notices reach staff in game, the server console, and Discord through DiscordSRV. Configurable, so a busy server can dial down to only what matters.

history

Useful from day one

It can draw on records your server has already been keeping, so it is worth something the day it is installed rather than months later.

light

Costs you nothing

A small jar with no external dependencies and no third-party services. Nothing it does sits between a player and getting into the game.

Firmament informs staff decisions; it does not make them. Anything it surfaces is a starting point for a person to look at, never a conclusion.

ADMIN COMMANDS

Four commands.

/alts <player>

Review a player

Opens the account view. Works from console as text.

/firmament import

Build the history

Populates records from what the server already has. Run once, when you install.

/firmament stats

Status

What it currently holds and which optional integrations were found.

/firmament reload

Re-read config

Applies configuration changes without a restart.

Permissions: firmament.lookup to review players, firmament.admin for the rest, firmament.alerts to receive notices in game. All three expose player connection details — grant them the way you would grant console access.
